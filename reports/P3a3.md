## Interpretations and surprises

- The prior reserved replay population size is retained at 1000; the plan prospectively allowed extension within dev seeds only if required. No extension was needed. Primary stops strictly before replay range.
- Screen all four tier constructions, including interaction admission, rather than checking only market creation. This cannot use a policy outcome. Fatal 32-candidate exhaustion is distinguished from the existing successful best-candidate fallback.
- Stress retains the round-two fixed mixed-policy schedule on the ascending screened design list. The same population is used at both concurrencies, run separately.
- Design replay retains historical distinct-seed behavior: next admissible entry on a hash collision with primary. Fresh primary/replay lists are disjoint.
- Two pre-policy review issues were corrected with prospective tests: substring-based freeze approval and missing same-seed design replay avoidance. Both corrections preceded every round-three policy run.
- More concurrency did not reduce measured stress wall time. The host deadline remains unchanged; no causal daemon-failure explanation is claimed.
- The known seed1000075 price-domain failure was the only construction rejection in the measured ranges. The clean false-flag partial credit remains a limitation of the frozen scoring rule.
