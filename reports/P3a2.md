## Interpretations, surprises and limits

The prompt's stress population was interpreted prospectively as 5000 at **each** concurrency, not 5000 combined. Repeated design cases use fresh containers; these are load tests, not 5000 independent market seeds or model evaluations. Scientific completeness of a recorded policy zero is separate from infrastructure health; cleanup failure still fails the gate. Optional probe telemetry does not replace the terminating transfer's status. The amendment was committed before measurements to keep documentary bytes source-bound; the transport correction has its own pre-rerun addendum.

Initial 10 prospective core tests preceded implementation. Additional freeze tests recorded 15 failures and 9 passes before the missing guards were fixed. Later review tests were added alongside fixes; some first ran after their concurrent fix, including the stale-boundary case documented in `logs/p3a2_stale_boundary_before.txt`. No claim is made that every later test initially failed. The first full suite also exposed two audit-exit-status regressions, which were corrected using the captured trace status and rechecked with real containers. The final corrected source-bound suite passes all 537 tests.

The late transport-status masking defect was surprising despite zero observed timeouts in the first stress runs; synthetic fault injection found it. Repeating both stress runs after correction avoids stale-source evidence. The pre-existing admission failure was the scientific blocker and was not fixed within an infrastructure-only authorization.

One numerical-regression initialization used an invalid uppercase archive label and was rejected before any rollout. The failed launch log is retained. Valid lowercase run labels were then used; canonical uppercase environment summary filenames are exclusive, byte-identical copies verified in `results/p3a2_regression_summary_aliases.json` and by independent review. The committed seed list and all outcomes stayed unchanged.

The deadline remains **60 seconds**. Final C8 recorded 102889 calls, maximum transfer 7.732623264s and daemon-probe latency 2.056794750s. Final C12 recorded 102889 calls, maximum transfer 30.713848154s and probe latency 3.548358067s. Both had zero host timeouts and zero failed probes. These observations do not justify increasing the deadline or establish the cause of the historical stall. Driver wall time includes host generation, integrity checks and evidence I/O; it is not solver latency.
